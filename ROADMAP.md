# Three-year roadmap (source-aligned)

**Dates:** October 2026–October 2029. **North star:** accountable for an AI platform's performance, economics, resilience, security and business outcomes.

## Year 1 · Oct 2026–Oct 2027 · Credible Lead AI Systems Engineer

**Pillars**
- **Distributed systems:** concurrency, async execution, caching, idempotency, queues/streaming, consistency, databases, failure recovery, Kubernetes architecture.
- **Production LLM engineering:** inference serving (for example, vLLM), batching, KV caching, model routing, latency/cost optimization, evaluations.
- **Agent reliability:** context management, long-running workflows, bounded tool permissions, human approvals, traces, evaluations.

**Evidence gate:** one substantial end-to-end AI system with architecture, operating SLOs, measured cost/latency, failure analyses and a technical review. Mentor 1–2 people where opportunities permit.

## Year 2 · Oct 2027–Oct 2028 · Staff / AI Architect scope

**Pillars**
- Reusable multi-tenant platform: isolation, RBAC, service contracts and reusable APIs.
- AI security and governance: prompt injection, tool auth, data protection, auditability and evaluation.
- Technical strategy: ADRs, build-vs-buy, migration plans, budgets, architectural standards.

**Evidence gate:** an AI capability reused by two teams/products; influence 3–5 engineers technically where possible; present quarterly decisions and trade-offs to senior stakeholders.

## Year 3 · Oct 2028–Oct 2029 · Principal Architect / Engineering Leader

**Pillars**
- 12–18-month strategy, standards and cross-portfolio technical priorities.
- Executive communication about risk, economics, reliability and delivery.
- Enterprise AI platform governance, mentoring, hiring/capacity/investment input.

**Evidence gate:** own architecture and measured outcome of a business-critical platform or portfolio; demonstrate organization-level impact. A job title or salary is not guaranteed.

## Supporting habits

- Continue DSA and clean service implementation for product-company interviews; practice concurrency and architecture interviews.
- Build six documented career stories: design, incident/failure, cost or latency improvement, disagreement/trade-off, leadership, business outcome.
- Read AI papers selectively; turn promising ideas into experiments rather than accumulating links.
- End each quarter with an architecture review and evidence-backed skill assessment.

## Interview capability

| Dimension | Required proof |
|---|---|
| System design | Load, scaling, isolation, recovery, data ownership, economics |
| AI architecture | RAG vs fine-tuning; serving; agent orchestration; evaluations |
| Coding | DSA, concurrency, clean maintainable services |
| Production | SLOs, incidents, observability, secure deployments |
| Leadership | Reviews, mentoring, cross-team influence |
| Business | ROI, build-vs-buy, prioritization, risk |
